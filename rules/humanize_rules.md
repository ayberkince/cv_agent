# ANTI-AI DETECTION & HUMAN CADENCE PROTOCOL

## 1. CORE WRITING METRICS (FORCE HIGH PERPLEXITY & BURSTINESS)
*   **Perplexity (Word Choice Unpredictability):** Use raw, industry-specific technical vocabulary, exact library functions, or mathematical frameworks. Never use generic corporate template filler words.
*   **Burstiness (Sentence Length Variance):** Intentionally break uniform sentence patterns. Mix very short, blunt clauses (4–6 words) with deeply complex, semi-colon-separated technical sequences within the same section.

---

## 2. THE LINGUISTIC BLACKLIST (DEATH WORDS)
You are strictly forbidden from using any of the following phrases anywhere in the document. Their inclusion will trigger pipeline validation failures:
*   **Forbidden Openings/Transitions:** *complemented by, eager to apply, proven ability, passion for, testament to, looking to leverage, dedicated to.*
*   **Forbidden Corporate Verbs:** *leverage, foster, streamline, delve, paving the way, driven by, spearhead, interface.*
*   **Forbidden Fillers:** *robust, insights, actionable insights, seamless, synergy, dynamic, innovative, seasoned, track record.*

---

## 3. PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY RE-ENGINEERING
*   **The Ban:** Completely ban the standard AI summary formula: *"M.Sc. Mathematics candidate with a strong foundation in X, complemented by Y, eager to apply Z."*
*   **The Human Architecture:** Write exactly three sentences using a direct, blunt, and slightly asymmetrical cadence:
    1.  *Sentence 1 (Blunt Fact):* State exactly who you are, your current location/academic state, and hard technical tooling. No flattering adjectives.
    2.  *Sentence 2 (Quantitative Proof):* State a heavy, factual metric or operational scale from your background.
    3.  *Sentence 3 (Immediate Objective):* State a direct, plain-English corporate objective without fluffy aspirations.

---

## 4. BULLET POINT VARIATION & CADENCE SHIFTING
To break the highly predictable "Action Verb + Task + Outcome" pattern that AI detectors flag instantly, enforce these style shifts across all Project and Experience lists:

### A. First-Word Diversity (Stop Repeating "Engineered / Developed")
Do not open every bullet point with a dominant, textbook action verb. Vary the grammatical starting points naturally:
*   **Past Tense Verbs:** *Orchestrated, Fine-tuned, Rolled out, Handled, Deployed, Programmed, Restructured, Executed.*
*   **Prepositional Openings:** *Through custom SQL pipelines...* / *By shifting calculations to NumPy...*
*   **Conversational Additions:** Occasionally start a minor secondary bullet point with casual connecting fragments like *“Also handled...”* or *“Plus managed...”*

### B. Structural Asymmetry & Conversational Injections
*   **Length Variance:** In a 3-bullet block, ensure one bullet is a long, deeply descriptive dual-clause statement separated by a semicolon, while another is a blunt, single-sentence implementation truth.
*   **Human Contractions:** To naturally mimic human speech patterns, inject exactly one professional contraction per major career/project block (e.g., *didn't, couldn't, wasn't, it's*).
*   **Metric Texturing:** Avoid making every single measurement look like a clean marketing metric ("50+ associates", "10k+ loops"). Blend hard numbers with natural human qualitative scaling terms:
    *   *Change:* "50+ logistics associates" ➔ "dozens of logistics associates" or "a floor cohort of 50 team members"
    *   *Change:* "10k+ stochastic loops" ➔ "thousands of simulation iterations"

## FORBIDDEN FIRST‑PERSON PRONOUNS
Never use "I", "we", "my", "our", "me", "us" in the resume. 
Use implied subject or passive voice where natural. 
Example: Instead of "I built a dashboard", write "Built a dashboard" or "Dashboard built in Streamlit".

## SENTENCE FRAGMENTS (NATURAL)
1-2 bullets per resume can be fragments (2-5 words, no verb).
Example: "Dashboard in Streamlit." or "Team lead for 50+."

# ABSOLUTE RULE: NO FIRST-PERSON
Never use: I, me, my, mine, we, us, our, I'm, I've, I'll, I'd.
Use implied subject, passive voice, or noun phrases.
Example: Instead of "I built a dashboard" → "Built a dashboard" or "Dashboard built in Streamlit".
